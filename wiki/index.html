African Music Curriculum

A run of classes on African popular music, taught in the order below. The order is for teaching, not lineage: a line on the map means a documented transmission, and nearness or a shared feel does not make one. These six are on the map now, without playlists or course numbers yet.

OrderPlanned ClassWhereRecorded Style Took Shape
1Congolese RumbaKinshasa and Brazzavillelate 1940s–50s
2RaïOran and western Algeria1970s–80s; sung since the 1920s
3TaarabZanzibar and the Swahili coastrecorded from 1928; performed earlier
4MbaqangaJohannesburg townships1960s
5MbalaxDakarlate 1970s–80s
6Ethio-JazzAddis Ababa, by way of New Yorklate 1960s–70s

Next Group

Egyptian Concert Song (on the map as the source of Taarab), Mandé Kora and Jeli Traditions, Benga, Chimurenga, Gnawa, and Morna.

About This Wiki

It is unlinked from the app and asks not to be indexed. The catalog is refreshed from the workbook with tools/wiki-export.py; the handbook, art guide and cover rules are edited by hand under wiki/. Nothing here is private, but none of it is for the public either: it is the working reference.
