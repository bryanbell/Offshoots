Add to Music Atlas

Found something worth reading, watching or hearing about a style in the atlas? Send it in. Everything is read before it goes on, and the best of it ends up under Go deeper.

This page is set up for you. The button below files what you send as your pick. Keep it to yourself: anyone with this page can send things in your name.

On a computer

Drag this button to your bookmarks bar.

Music Atlas

Then, on any page you want to suggest, click it. A small form opens with the link and title already filled in. Pick the styles it belongs on and send.

No bookmarks bar? Turn it on from the View menu in Safari, Chrome or Firefox.

On iPhone or iPad

Copy the link from Safari or the app you're in, then open the form and paste it.

Open the form

Or, inside Music Atlas, tap Suggest something at the bottom of a style's Go deeper list. That style is filled in for you.

Get the Music Atlas Shortcut

Add it to the Share menu

A Shortcut puts Music Atlas in the Share menu, so you can send a page without copying anything. It takes a couple of minutes, once.

  1. Open the Shortcuts app and tap +.
  2. Add the action URL Encode. Tap its input and choose Shortcut Input.
  3. Add the action Text and type https://musicatlas.us/suggest#u= then, right after it, insert URL Encoded Text from the variables above the keyboard.
  4. Add the action Open URLs. Its input should be Text.
  5. Tap the name at the top, call it Music Atlas, then turn on Show in Share Sheet and set it to receive URLs.

Now in Safari: Share, then Music Atlas.
